Conjunctiva Scan

Non-invasive anemia screening via palpebral conjunctiva color analysis.

Capture Instructions
1
Bright Natural Light

Stand near a window. Avoid yellow indoor bulbs or dark shadows.

2
Expose the Conjunctiva

Gently pull your lower eyelid down until the pink/white inner tissue is visible.

3
Stable Focus

Hold the camera 6-8 inches away. Ensure the inner eyelid is sharp and not blurry.

Note: This tool is for screening purposes. Pallor (paleness) can be an indicator, but it does not replace a clinical blood test.
